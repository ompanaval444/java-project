About This Opportunity

Department of CSE AI & ML presents ROOKERY HACKATHON - KEEP BUILDING! Got an idea? Build anything. Get funded. Sell it after. Join us for an 8-hour open innovation hackathon with NO fixed problem statement: just your idea, your team, and the freedom to build. Highlights: • $20-$30 seed funding per team • 1st Place Prize + Cash Prizes for Top 3 • $500 worth of AI credits • Winning projects can go live on Dropcode and reach real users!
